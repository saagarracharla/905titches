About 905titches

Where craftsmanship meets technology in the art of custom fabrication.

The Journey

What started as a passion for embroidery has evolved into a full-service custom fabrication studio. 905titches began with a simple idea: combine traditional craftsmanship with modern technology to create something truly unique.

From our first embroidered hoodie to advanced laser engraving systems, we've continuously expanded our capabilities while maintaining an unwavering commitment to quality and precision. Every project is an opportunity to push boundaries and explore new possibilities.

Today, we're more than just a fabrication shop—we're a maker lab where creativity and technology converge. Whether it's intricate threadwork, precision laser cutting, or vibrant DTG printing, we bring your vision to life with meticulous attention to detail.

Our Evolution

Established as a complete fabrication studio, combining traditional craftsmanship with advanced technology for unlimited creative possibilities.
